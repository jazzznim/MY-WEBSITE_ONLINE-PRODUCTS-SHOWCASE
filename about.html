ABOUT GLOW BEAUTY

A student-created beauty concept.

GLOW Beauty is an original fictional cosmetics brand made for an HTML and CSS web development activity. It is designed as an online product showcase where visitors can view creative beauty product concepts.

Our theme is inspired by flowers, soft colors, and simple everyday beauty. The website aims to present product information in a neat, organized, and user-friendly way.

Our purpose

To practice basic web design by creating a clear product catalog, attractive layouts, and working navigation using HTML5 and CSS3 only.
